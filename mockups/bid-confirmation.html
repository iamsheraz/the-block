Why this shape

The success panel reconfirms position relative to reserve — not just "you won the click." A buyer who is the high bidder but below reserve has placed a marker, not a winning bid; making that explicit prevents the trap of celebrating too early.

Both actions are equal-weight buttons because returning to the list and inspecting the bid are both normal next moves at this point in the flow.
